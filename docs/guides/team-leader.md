# Relay CRM: guide for Team Leaders

Everything in the Sales rep guide applies to your own deals. On top of that you run a team.

## Watching your team
- **Board** shows your team's open deals by stage (filter by rep, source, health, age). Cards show health and warnings; no forecast is shown.
- **Stage pages** list one stage in full with filters, sorting, 10/25/50 per page and bulk actions (change owner, snooze, close as Lost, export).
- **Monitoring** shows each rep: assigned and active leads against their cap, deals by stage, last interaction, next action, won, lost and why. Click a rep for the full profile.
- **Today**: open any team member's Today and **assign a task** (a next step) to a rep. They are notified.

## Verifying work
Open a deal, then **Verify** or **Reject** (with a reason) any tick. Rejecting a tick on a stage the deal already left flags the deal **Review needed** for you and the rep; press **Review done** when you have looked at it. A **Proof missing** warning also notifies you.

## Moving deals
- You cannot tick or move forward on deals you do not own.
- **Skip / override** (reason always required) works on your team's deals, not your own. Skipped criteria are recorded as "Overridden by <you>".
- **Reopen** a lost deal into the stage it was lost from (reason). **Delete / Restore** (reason) never erases anything; see **Deleted**.
- Reassign deals one by one or in bulk (reason). The Lead Pool is yours to assign when a lead is waiting.

## Approvals and prices
- Up to 10% discount on your team's deals needs a reason; above 10% needs an Admin.
- A change of deal value above 10% goes to an Admin. Approvals from your reps arrive under **Approvals**.
- Editing an invoice is a request to an Admin.

## Transfers
**Transfers** lets you ask to take a deal from another team, or give one to them. An Admin decides. The other Team Leader can add a comment. When approved, the previous owner loses access immediately.

## Exports
**Exports** needs a reason, covers your team only, and every export is logged where you and Admins can see it.
