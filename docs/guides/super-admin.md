# Relay CRM: guide for Super admins

You can do everything an Admin can, and a reason is optional (never required) on your actions, though adding one helps the history. A few things are yours alone or need extra care.

- **Records you own are hidden** from Team Leaders and reps. Admins can see them.
- **Fit checklist and probabilities**: you decide who counts as a good fit and what each stage's probability is. **Stage metrics** (on profiles and the board panel) show the *measured* win rate next to the configured probability so you can adjust with evidence.
- **Pipeline publishing and rollback** are yours as well as the Admins'. Publish only when the draft check is clean; read the impact preview first.
- **Last Super admin**: the system never lets the last Super admin be demoted or deactivated.
- **Audit trail**: the history is append-only and checked every night; a broken chain notifies Super admins. See `docs/adr/0003`.
- **Pilot and go-live**: see `docs/runbooks/pilot-golive.md`.
