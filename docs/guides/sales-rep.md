# Relay CRM: guide for Sales reps (User)

One page. Everything you do is saved with who and when.

## Your day
1. Open **Today**. **Your move** lists what you owe the customer; **Waiting on them** lists what they owe you.
2. Finish a step with **Done**. You must set the next step straight away: a deal is never left without one.
3. **Snooze +2 days** moves a step. There is no limit, every snooze is counted.
4. Customer replied? Press **They replied**. Chased them? Press **Nudged**.
5. Red badges: **Nudge due** (waiting 2+ days), **Stale** (7+ days without any logged activity), **At risk** (next step overdue).

## A new lead
- **New lead** asks for company, contact (phone or email), source, a next step and its due date.
- While you type, the page checks for the same company. An **exact** match (website, phone, email) blocks you. A **similar** name warns you; **Create anyway** needs a reason.
- The lead is assigned automatically (rules, then round-robin, within each rep's lead cap). If nobody can take it, it waits in the Lead Pool and your Team Leader is told.

## Taking a deal forward
Open the deal. The **checklist** shows what this stage needs.
- **Tick** a criterion only after its proof exists (an activity, a document, a filled field). The page tells you what is missing.
- In **Lead** you also answer the four fit questions (website real, phone correct, client interested, company genuine) on the deal and tick "Fits the target customer profile". In **Qualification** you write down the client's problem (pain point) and how our product solves it, and schedule the meeting.
- Move forward one stage at a time, only when every criterion is ticked. **Only the deal's owner ticks and moves forward.**
- **Revision** is mandatory for every deal. A second round (**Revision 2**) needs a reason.
- **Move back** needs a reason. Skipping stages is for Team Leaders and above.
- Your Team Leader may **verify** or **reject** a tick. A rejected tick is removed and you are notified.
- If proof disappears later (an activity or document is deleted) the tick shows **Proof missing**. Add the proof again and the warning clears by itself.

## Logging what happened
**Log activity** asks: type, when, did the customer respond (calls, emails, meetings), what was discussed, and the next step. You can edit your own activity for 24 hours (with a reason); every version is kept. Nobody edits someone else's activity.

## Prices and discounts
- Pick products from the catalog; the deal value is worked out from the lines.
- A change of value up to 5% is free; up to 10% needs a reason; above 10% needs your Team Leader's approval.
- Any discount needs your Team Leader's approval; above 10% (or below the floor price) needs an Admin. The line shows **Discount awaiting approval** and the value does not change until it is approved.

## Closing
- **Close as Lost**: choose a reason from the list.
- A deal reaches **Won** when it enters **Onboarding**; finish the onboarding checklist there.
- Mistakes right after an edit: use **Undo** (10 seconds).

## Good to know
You see your own deals. Other teams' companies show only as "exists in another team". Every export needs a reason and is logged.
